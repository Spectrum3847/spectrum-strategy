The relay checks every few hours for a newer signed build of the relay and installs it, then restarts. It never installs a file whose signature or checksum does not match, and never goes back to an older build. Updates need the relay to start at login.

Sign in

Sign in once with your team account. The relay answers requests as that member and keeps a refresh token on this device.

Model

A GGUF chat model. The relay downloads it on the next check and deletes the previous one.

If your computer blocked the relay

The relay is not signed with an Apple or Microsoft certificate, so each system asks once.

macOS

Right-click the relay file, choose Open, then press Open. Or open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and press Open Anyway next to the relay.

Windows

When SmartScreen says it protected your PC, press More info, then Run anyway.
